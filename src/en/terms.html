3. Health and safety

The beep test is a maximal-effort test: you keep running until you can no longer keep up with the beeps.

  • Talk to a doctor before maximal exercise if you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, an injury or another health concern, if you’re pregnant, or if you haven’t exercised for a long time.
  • Warm up, run on a non-slip surface with enough space behind each line, and wear suitable shoes.
  • Stop immediately if you feel chest pain, dizziness, unusual shortness of breath or pain.
  • Children and teenagers should take the test under the supervision of an adult, such as a teacher or coach.
  • ShuttleIQ is not a medical device. VO₂max, forecasts, norms and AI tips are estimates for training, not medical advice or a diagnosis.
